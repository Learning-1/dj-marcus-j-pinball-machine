# DJ MARCUS J’S PINBALL MACHINE

## Play on Quest

1. Stand in front of the cabinet. Grab the red plunger to open the initials panel. Use the plus and minus buttons to choose three letters, then select **PLAY**.
2. Grab the plunger again. Pull straight back and release. A longer pull launches the ball harder.
3. Point at either red cabinet button and squeeze the index trigger to fire its flipper. You can also press downward over the button with your controller.
4. Play all three balls. The center drain counts one ball when it falls in. The scoreboard shows your final score and the five best finishes in this world instance.
5. Grab the plunger again to play another game. The table reuses your initials until a different player takes over or you leave the world. The high scores clear when everyone leaves the instance.

## Play on Windows

Click the cabinet buttons or use **A** for the left flipper and **D** for the right. Press **Space** to claim the table. Enter initials using the panel or keyboard letters, then click **PLAY**. Hold and release **Space** to launch. You can also grab and pull the plunger with the mouse.

## Scoring

- Bumper hit: 100 points.
- Slingshot hit: 50 points.
- Stand-up target: 150 points. Light all three blue inserts to ready the bonus. Cross either lit rollover to collect 500 points.
- Ordinary rollover: 75 points.
- Optional red drop targets: 200 points each, plus 300 for clearing all three. They reset with the next ball.

## Install in a world

Add a **BS Snippet** component to an empty Unity GameObject at the origin and use the slug `dj-marcus-j-pinball-machine` once the listing is available. Set **position** to the floor point beneath the machine and **rotation** to its heading around the Y axis. Leave clear space in front of both buttons and the plunger. Turn **drop-targets** off if you prefer the open right-side playfield. Place one machine per world. If the latest build does not appear, leave and rejoin the world.

For release hosting and version updates, see the included Readme.
